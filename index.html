    How to play

    Shop smart, ride fast

    • Swipe left or right to change lane. Swipe up or tap Jump to hop over carts and potholes. On a computer use the arrow keys and Space.
    • Green rings are on your shopping list. Grab them all to deliver the order.
    • Gold rings are bonus vegetables: more points for a healthier pot. Red rings are not on your list and waste money.
    • Danfo buses can't be jumped. Switch lanes! Three hits and the keke breaks down.
    • Power-up crates: Magnet pulls items to you, Haggle halves prices, Spare tyre saves you from one crash, 2× coins doubles coins.
    • Fail a level and you lose a plate. Plates refill by one every 20 minutes.
