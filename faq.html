Getting started

What is Meuro?
Meuro is an African food and wellness ecosystem. Meuro AI helps you understand everyday meals and plan around your goals and budget, verified nutritionists give you human support, Meuro Kitchen caters for organisations and events, and Meuro Foods is developing nutrient-dense African-made products.
Who is Meuro for?
Anyone who wants to eat better with the food they already know: busy professionals, students, parents, people managing a health condition with a professional, athletes and organisations feeding teams.
Does it cost anything?
Meuro AI is free during beta. Nutritionists set their own session fees, which are shown before you book. Catering is quoted per event.

Meuro AI

Which dishes can it recognise?
It is built for everyday African meals such as jollof, fried rice, efo riro, egusi, okra, amala, gbegiri, pepper soup, moi moi, akara and more. Recognition improves as more people use it.
How accurate are the estimates?
No. Estimates are informational and vary with ingredients, cooking method and portion size. Use them as a guide, and speak with a nutritionist for anything clinical.
How does the budget planner work?
You choose a goal, a budget in naira, a number of days and the food you have. Meuro builds daily meals with an estimated cost and protein range, using affordable additions where needed.

Nutritionists

How are nutritionists verified?
Professionals submit their credentials when they join. Only those whose qualifications are reviewed appear as verified on Meuro.
Can I meet someone in person?
Yes. You can filter by location and consultation type to meet online or find support near you.
Which conditions do experts cover?
PCOS and women's health, diabetes, kidney health, heart health, gut health, weight management, sports nutrition and prenatal nutrition.

Privacy & safety

What happens to my data?
Sensitive health information is only used with your consent, and you stay in control of what you share with an expert.
Does Meuro AI replace my doctor?
No. Meuro AI does not diagnose or treat disease. For medical concerns, speak with a qualified health professional.
What happens to my Market Run account?
We keep your rider name and the email address you sign up with. Your email never appears on the leaderboard. We only send meal plans and offers if you tick the box, and you can opt out or ask us to delete your account at any time.
Do you track how I use the site?
Only if you tap OK on the analytics notice. We then use Google Analytics to count visits and game activity, such as which dishes are picked and how many rides are finished, so we can improve Meuro. It does not receive your name or email. Tap No thanks and nothing is recorded; to change your mind later, clear this site's data in your browser and the notice will appear again.

Meuro Kitchen & Foods

How large an event can Meuro Kitchen cater?
From small team lunches to large events. Share your group size, date and location and the team will prepare an indicative menu and quote.
When will Meuro Foods launch?
Products are in development. Join the waitlist on the Meuro Foods page to hear about early access.

Still have a question?

A verified nutritionist can help with anything specific to your health.
