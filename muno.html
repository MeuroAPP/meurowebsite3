Muno is food, not medicine. If your child is losing weight, not growing well, or is often unwell, please see a doctor or talk to a Meuro nutritionist first.

Allergy information: contains soya and groundnuts (peanuts). Not suitable for children with these allergies. Muno is not an infant formula or a breast-milk substitute.

Storage: keep refrigerated. Shake well before drinking.
