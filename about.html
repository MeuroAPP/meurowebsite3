African. Scientific. Human.

Meuro is building a connected African food, nutrition and wellness ecosystem, linking practical technology, better products, food service and trusted professionals.

Your body is the vehicle that carries you to every goal you have.

Why Meuro exists

Meuro was started to make food a healing tool. Most people across Africa eat without knowing what their food is doing for their health, and good nutrition advice rarely speaks to the meals on our tables.

We help people understand their food in relation to their health goals, and we connect them to the professionals and products that support better health.
